Build a make.fun entry for [Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family Scene](https://make.fun/bounty/u-shino-make-the-ai-judge-feel-proud-a-family-scene-ee2e3fa2) from scratch in this session.

- Task: 1-2 lines, max 280 chars; program writes `/output/entry.txt`.
- Judge criteria, most important first:
  1. Short family scene, real family on the page in 1-2 lines, warm enough to make a proud judge smile.

  3. Light, recognisable humour: small turn / wry line a real family would laugh at.
  4. 1-2 lines used well: one whole scene in a breath, no word wasted.
  5. Specific and fresh: one telling detail or unexpected angle, not a greeting-card line.

  anchor/rung house texts from the kit (byte-identical reproduction takes no prize).
- main.ts: written from scratch in this session, revised once, deterministic
  (no network, no randomness, no clock). Test twice to different output dirs;
  outputs must be byte-identical.
- Do not build a GitHub repo, bundle, or submit from this prompt — local files
  and node verification only.
